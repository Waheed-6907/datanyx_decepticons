How it works

From a free-text report to a reviewed, traceable decision in five steps.

1ReadReport text, checklist or voice; findings + source sentences extracted.
2Two judgesWHO rule engine and an ML model stage the cyst independently.
3Self-doubtDisagreement, missing findings or poor images trigger "review required".
4Explain + planEvidence, management options and referral priority, in plain words.
5Doctor decidesConfirm or override; every decision is logged and builds the cyst's digital twin.
